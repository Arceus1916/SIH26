## 1. Project Planning Rubric

1. **What is it?**  
   Project ORCA (Ocean Remote-Sensing & Tactical Marine Intelligence Copilot) is a multi-stakeholder maritime decision-support platform fusing Sentinel-3, MODIS Aqua, and INCOIS oceanographic telemetry with RAG AI copilot intelligence across 50+ worldwide ports.

2. **Who is it for, and what does it do for them?**  
   - **Fishermen:** Potential Fishing Zones (PFZ), fuel payback calculation, border & IMBL safety geofencing.
   - **Researchers:** Sentinel-3 SLSTR thermal fronts, SST gradient anomalies, chlorophyll plumes, NetCDF-4 hydrographic data export.
   - **Coastal Authorities:** IAMSAR leeway drift search datum, 61-day monsoon fishing ban compliance, marine sanctuary geofences.
   - **Disaster Management:** Tropical cyclone cones, gale wind radii, coastal storm surge projections.
   - **Maritime Operators:** Under-keel clearance (UKC), dynamic draft analysis, tidal arrival windows.

3. **What sets it apart?**  
   - Real-time thermal wind particle canvas, bathymetric contours, and interactive waypoint placement.
   - 100% Palette A nautical design system ("Chart Paper" light & "Night Bridge" dark).
   - Zero emojis, zero generic gradients, precision tactical instruments.

4. **Visual Hook:**  
   Opening with sonar bathymetric contours pulsing outward from the Project ORCA logo, transitioning seamlessly into tactical mission console with live satellite telemetry.

5. **Audio & Sound Design:**  
   Cinematic nautical pulse with tactile mechanical interface clicks, subtle sub-bass thud for scene transitions, and clean sonar ping accents from bundled SFX assets.
